# Challenge: Data Management and Analysis for SKA and JWST

Managing the large volumes of data produced by the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) and James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) requires advanced data management and analytical skills.

Important skills and processes include:

1. **Data storage and management:** Store, organise and back up large datasets.
2. **Programming:** Use Python, SQL and other programming languages to process data.
3. **Data cleaning:** Identify incomplete, duplicated or inaccurate information.
4. **Statistics:** Identify patterns, trends and relationships.
5. **Data visualisation:** Present findings using charts, graphs and dashboards.
6. **Machine learning and AI:** Help identify patterns in large and complex datasets.
7. **Data security:** Protect research data and control access.
8. **Data quality management:** Ensure that information remains accurate and reliable.

Long-term storage, regular backups, documentation, standardised procedures and collaboration between researchers will help ensure that scientific data remains useful for future research.
