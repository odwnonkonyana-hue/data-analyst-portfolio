# Reflection

A data analyst needs technical skills such as Excel, SQL, statistics, data cleaning, data visualisation and data interpretation. Communication, critical thinking and problem-solving are also important because analysts must explain their findings clearly.

To develop my portfolio projects, I need to improve my practical experience with Excel, SQL and Power BI. I would also like to develop Python skills to work with larger datasets.

My career goal is to become a skilled data analyst who can use data to identify trends, solve problems and support informed decision-making.
